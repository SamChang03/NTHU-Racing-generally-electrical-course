# NTHU Racing — General Electrical Courses

Lecture notes for new members of the **electrical systems group of NTHU Racing** (National Tsing Hua University Formula Student team). The series builds the basic electrical-engineering background needed before working on the car's electrical systems.

Author: 張育碩 (Yu-Shuo Chang)

## Lectures

| # | Topic | Slides | Main contents |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Logic Design | [PDF](01.Logic%20Design.pdf) | Analog vs. digital, Boolean algebra, DeMorgan's law, truth tables, Karnaugh maps, logic gates, combinational vs. sequential logic, latches and flip-flops |
| 02 | RLC | [PDF](02.RLC.pdf) | Voltage and current, resistors, inductors, capacitors, DC vs. AC circuits, impedance |
| 03 | Circuit Analysis | [PDF](03.CKT%20Analysis.pdf) | Circuit models, Kirchhoff's laws, voltage division, node-voltage method, source transformations, Thévenin equivalents, maximum power transfer |
| 04 | Operational Amplifier | [PDF](04.Operational%20Amplifier.pdf) | Ideal op-amp and virtual short, difference and bridge amplifiers, voltage follower, inverting and non-inverting amplifiers, comparator, integrator, differentiator |
| 05 | Physics of Semiconductor | [PDF](05.Physic%20of%20Semiconductor.pdf) | Carriers and doping, bandgap, mass-action law, drift and diffusion current, PN junction under bias, diode models, small-signal model, half- and full-wave rectifiers |
| 06 | Microelectronics | *coming soon* | |

Lecture 01 includes exercises and a homework set. The lectures are meant to be taken in order: 01–03 cover circuit basics, and 04–05 build on them toward analog and semiconductor devices.

## Suggested background

General physics (electricity and magnetism) and basic calculus. No prior EE coursework is assumed.
